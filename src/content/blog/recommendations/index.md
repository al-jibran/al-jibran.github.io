This is a growing list of resources I have benefited from immensely.

If you want to contribute to this list, feel free to message me on LinkedIn.

> **Note on free resources**
>
> The resources below are available for free at the time of writing. I believe knowledge should be open, free, and easily accessible and these are some of the best examples I have found.
>
> If you benefit from them, support the authors however you can: follow their work, buy their content, buy them a coffee, or tell other people about what they made.
>
> If any of these are no longer free, let me know by raising an issue on GitHub or by messaging me on LinkedIn.

## Free Resources

## AI

### [First Break AI](https://cohort.bubblnet.com/)

A free, community-driven cohort for getting into AI engineering by actually building things. It shows you the bigger picture by running an AI model locally before diving deeper including the model supply chain, LLMs, inference and building projects with AI.

### [Elements of AI: Building AI](https://buildingai.elementsofai.com/)

A MOOC provided by the University of Helsinki that teaches the fundamentals of ML and building AI systems including probability, ML algorithms, neural networks. A good starting point before diving into deeper AI material.

## Design Patterns

### [Game Programming Patterns](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/)

While written in the context of game development, every developer can benefit from it. In fact, seeing the patterns being used in context makes them stick better than just reading theory.

## Software Architecture

### [metapatterns.io](https://metapatterns.io/)

A very useful map of architectural patterns and the relationships between them.

> You can rarely make a product in a pure architectural style, and the chances for it to survive undistorted over years are negligible. Architectural Metapatterns is all about patterns and their relations. It generalizes hundreds of individual patterns into several wider classes (metapatterns) each of which can be applied to a local or distributed system to change its properties in a certain way

## Web Development

### [Full-Stack Open](https://fullstackopen.com/en)

The best resource on learning Full-Stack Javascript development, bar none. It covers React, Node.js, REST APIs, testing, GraphQL, TypeScript, CI/CD, containers, and more. This is an in-depth course that contains really good explanations and exercises.

### [javascript.info](https://javascript.info/)

It is one of the best references for learning modern JavaScript properly, from the language fundamentals to DOM, events, async programming, modules, browser APIs, and advanced topics.
